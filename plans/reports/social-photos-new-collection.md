# Social Photos Design Report

## Overview
- Prompt: social photos for new collection
- Brand: Velura
- Platforms: Instagram post 1080x1080 and Instagram story 1080x1920
- Variations: 3 concepts, 6 HTML source files, 6 exported PNGs
- Style: fitting-room atelier, product-led fashion editorial

## Ideas Generated
1. **Measured Close** - Launch announcement using the campaign image, dark overlay, and measuring tape rails.
2. **Soft Shape, Clean Lines** - Product collage for carousel/feed use, pairing new and hero products with a light blueprint field.
3. **Find Your Line** - Material-led story/post with swatches and a quiet fit message.

## Design Decisions
- Color palette: ink #151110, shell #F2E6E1, atelier cream #F6F0EA, rosewood #B9746A, brass #A7895F, blueprint #415266.
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond for restrained luxury headlines, DM Sans for short labels and CTA text.
- Layout: exact-size static HTML canvases, no scroll, with central safe-zone copy and product imagery as the focal point.
- Signature: measuring tape rails repeat across concepts to connect the collection to Velura's fit-first positioning.

## Why This Works
- The assets use real Velura imagery and product names rather than generic fashion placeholders.
- Text is intentionally low, large, and positioned inside safe zones for feed and story cropping.
- The measuring tape device makes the collection feel ownable without overcrowding the photographs.

## Recommendations
- Use Idea 1 for launch day.
- Use Idea 2 as a carousel cover paired with product detail slides.
- Use Idea 3 for story links, polls, or fit education.
